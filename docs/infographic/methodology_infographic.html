Methodology: dumpsite footprints 2016–2026 and siting check against the SWM Rules 2026

14 dumpsites in 10 Gujarat cities · six epochs · manual tracing in satellite imagery → area, perimeter and shape → trends → rule-by-rule siting test
14 sites · 6 epochs · 84 site-epochs
103 outlines · 10 of 12 siting rules testable
Common inputOutlinesWhat was waste, where, and when?
1Imagery and epochs
  • Google Earth Pro historical imagery (sub-metre)
  • Epochs 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024, 2026 (2025 image used for 2024 at 5 sites)
  • Automatic Sentinel-2 outlines tested and rejected: cleared ground looks like waste at 10 m
2Manual tracing
  • Waste footprint traced per site, part and epoch
  • Leachate pools, sheds, trommels and cleared ground excluded
  • “Remediated” pin = no waste that year (area 0)
3One master KML
  • 122 placemarks: 103 outlines + 19 pins
  • Names read as site · part · year (e.g. Surat LF-2(c) 2026)
  • Pilot on 5 sites: all geometries valid
Part AArea and perimeterHow did each footprint change?
4Project and merge
  • WGS84 → UTM zone 43N (EPSG:32643)
  • Parts of one site summed (Khajod a+b+c, Vadodara 2, Ahmedabad 2)
  • Missing part in a traced year = 0
5Area and perimeter
A = Σ area of parts (ha)
P = Σ perimeter (5 m simplified)
  • Edge uncertainty: ±2 m buffer
±U = [A+2 m − A−2 m] / 2
6Shape
Compactness = 4πA / P2
Shape index = P / 2√(πA)
Fractal dimension = 2 ln(P/4) / ln A
  • 1 = circle; lower compactness = irregular or fragmented
7Change and trend
  • Net change 2016→2026, largest extent and year
  • Theil–Sen slope (ha yr−1); Mann–Kendall exact P, n = 6
  • Class threshold = max(15%, 2 × edge uncertainty): expanding · contracting · rise and fall · cleared then regrowing
  • Land turnover: newly covered vs no longer waste (outline overlay)

9Open data layers
  • OpenStreetMap: rivers, ponds, roads, parks, wetlands, coast, protected areas
  • Overture buildings + GHSL 2018 residential cells
  • JRC / Copernicus 100-year flood map (90 m)
  • Aerodromes: OSM + OurAirports reference points
10Measure and judge
  • 2026 outline (2016 for comparison)
  • Edge-to-edge distance in a site-centred equidistant projection
  • Flood: ≥ 10% of outline inside = breach
  • Habitation: ≥ 5 homes in 200 m + imagery review

Quality controlChecks and outputs
11Reproducible
  • Python scripts re-run from the KML; tables byte-identical on re-run
  • Hand check in QGIS / Google Earth Pro (area, perimeter, distances)
12Independent checks
  • Geodesic vs UTM area: ≤ 0.65% difference
  • Siting distances recomputed by a second route: agree to 0.1 m
13Outputs
  • Per site and epoch: area, perimeter, shape, trend class
  • Site × rule verdict matrix (14 × 10 = 140 checks), 2016 vs 2026
