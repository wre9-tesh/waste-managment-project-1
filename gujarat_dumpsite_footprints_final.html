Gujarat Dumpsite Footprints, 2016–2026

14 dumpsites in 10 cities, traced by hand on Google Earth historical imagery at six epochs. Sites traced in parts (Surat LF-2 Khajod, Vadodara LF-2, Ahmedabad LF-2) are reported as one site: area and perimeter are the sum of their parts. Areas and perimeters computed in UTM zone 43N; perimeter after 5 m simplification.

Total waste footprint by city group

Sum of traced waste area per epoch. Major cities = the five most populous (Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Bhavnagar; 9 sites). Other cities = Gandhinagar, Jamnagar, Bhuj, Mehsana, Vapi (5 sites).

Results table

Theil–Sen slope is the median of all pairwise slopes, robust with six points. Mann–Kendall τ and exact p (permutation test, n = 6) are descriptive only; with six epochs even a perfectly steady trend reaches p ≈ 0.003 at best.

Perimeter and shape, 2016 vs 2026

Shape index = P / (2√(πA)), 1 for a circle, higher = more irregular. Compactness = 4πA/P², 1 for a circle, lower = more irregular. Fractal dimension = 2·ln(P/4)/ln(A), 1 for simple edges, towards 2 for convoluted edges.

How the numbers were made

  • Trajectory classes: Expanding (net growth > threshold, still near peak), Rise and fall (grew, then fell > threshold from an interior peak), Contracting (net decline > threshold), Cleared then regrowing (net decline > threshold, but 2026 above an interior low by more than the threshold), Cleared (no waste in 2026), Stable (within threshold). Threshold = max(15%, 2 × mean ±2 m buffer uncertainty).
  • Newly covered land = area in the 2026 outline not in the 2016 outline. Land not under waste in 2026 = everything ever outlined 2016–2026 minus the 2026 outline (cleared, biomined, built over, or re-shaped heaps).
  • Uncertainty: ±2 m buffer method (Paul et al. 2013, 2020). Repeatability test (blind re-trace) and Sentinel-2 agreement check are still to be run.
